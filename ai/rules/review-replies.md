# Replying to reviewers

- Never reply to a comment a human wrote on a pull request, issue, or review thread, and never resolve a human's thread. Bring the proposed answer to me in chat instead.
- Reply to a human only when I explicitly ask for that reply. The permission covers that one reply alone: ask again before replying to a human another time, even in the same thread or session.
- Automated reviewers (bots such as Klippy, Atlas, or Copilot) are the exception: reply to their findings and resolve their threads as the project's workflow allows.
